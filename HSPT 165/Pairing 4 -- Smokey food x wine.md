
To combat smokiness of the food you can fight with richness of wine. 

## Richness
- Richness in wine is described as big, powerful, brawney.
- Oak barrel fermentation and aging add richness to wine.
- Richness in food occurs from 
	- caramelization and concentration (broiling, roasting, grilling)
	- high-heat fat cooking (grilling, broiling)
	- low-heat, long cooking (baking, roasting, braising, crock pot, instant pot)
	- Smoking (fatty foods picking up the most smoked flavor)

## Oak vs SmOAKy

- These two goes well together! 
- flavor enriching impact of oak on wine and a flavor enriching impact of smoke on food.
