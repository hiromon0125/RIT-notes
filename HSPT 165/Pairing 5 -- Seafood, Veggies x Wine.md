
## Poaching

- to cook gently in a liquid in hot water (160 to 180° not bubbling)
- Steps
	- bring water to boil
	- reduce to 180°
	- add item to be poached
	- remove when it reach desired doneness

## Blanch

- Boiling with salt water then rapidly cool via ice bath
- To boiling then to shock(cool instantly)
