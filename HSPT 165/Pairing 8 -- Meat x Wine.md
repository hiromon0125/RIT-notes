==Balance is important==
A balance of flavor, intensity and texture(FIT) on the plate lends to better wine affinity. In other words, if you balance your FAT component, you balance your FIT!

==Fat acts as richness in food==
- As a condiment to bring out "peak-sweet"flavor in food (Chapter 2)
- In protein, fat. is the foundation of "meaty" richness in foods(Chapter 4)

==The "FAT" connection==
- fatty-rich foods have an intrinsic wine-loving flavor simply on their own (good quality olive oil, butter and foie gras)
- Fatty-rich foods have an amazing power to act as a "bridge ingredient" to boost other flavors in both food and wine.

==The dual abilities of fat==
- fat can capture and contain flavor(as in smoked foods like salmon, where the flavor is sealed in)
- Fat can also soften and enrich flavor(as in adding oil to vinegar to make an emulsion such as mayonnaise)
